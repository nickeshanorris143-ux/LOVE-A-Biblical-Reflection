Understanding Love Through Scripture

✿ · · · ✿ · · · ✿

Love is more than words or feelings. The Bible teaches us that love is reflected in how we treat one another, how we respond to challenges, and how we choose to act toward others.

Through 1 Corinthians 13:4–7, we can explore the qualities of love, understand what loving treatment should look like, and reflect on how we can demonstrate love in our own lives.

Love should be something we practice, not simply something we say.

Choose a section above to explore patience, respect, truth, and hope.

Love Is Patient

1 Corinthians 13:4

Charity suffereth long, and is kind; charity envieth not; charity vaunteth not itself, is not puffed up,

What This Teaches Us

Love involves patience, kindness, and humility. It does not depend on envy, pride, or the need to prove that we are better than others.

How We Can Show It

We can practice patience by listening, being considerate, and treating others kindly, even when things do not go exactly as we hoped.

Love Does Not Dishonor Others

1 Corinthians 13:5

Doth not behave itself unseemly, seeketh not her own, is not easily provoked, thinketh no evil;

What This Teaches Us

Love treats others with respect and consideration. It considers how our words and actions affect other people.

How We Can Show It

We can show respect by listening, speaking thoughtfully, respecting boundaries, and treating people with dignity.

Love Rejoices in the Truth

1 Corinthians 13:6

Rejoiceth not in iniquity, but rejoiceth in the truth;

What This Teaches Us

Love does not celebrate wrongdoing. It values honesty, fairness, and what is right.

How We Can Show It

We can show love by being honest, taking responsibility for our actions, and speaking truthfully with kindness.

Love Hopes and Endures

1 Corinthians 13:7

Beareth all things, believeth all things, hopeth all things, endureth all things.

What This Teaches Us

Love offers hope and encourages us to keep believing in what is good. It shows commitment through difficult times while recognizing the importance of healthy boundaries.

How We Can Show It

We can show hope by encouraging others, offering support, keeping our promises, and facing challenges with patience and understanding.
